CrayonNote Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026 · 한국어

ARNOA ("the developer") values the privacy of people who use the "CrayonNote" app ("the app"). This page explains how the app handles information.

1. Information the app collects

The app has no sign-up. It does not collect personal information such as your name, contact details or location, and it does not send such information to the developer's servers.

The diaries and memos you write (text and titles), your moods and weather choices, crayon strokes, stamps and stickers you make, your daily planner, and the photos and doodles you attach are stored only on your device (in the app's private storage). They are never sent to the developer or to any external server. They are deleted when you uninstall the app, and you can delete them yourself inside the app at any time.

Only the photos you choose through the system photo picker or your device's camera app are copied into the app. The app does not request separate photo or camera permissions.

The app does not use Google's automatic backup. If you tap "Export backup" in Settings, the app bundles your diaries, memos, planner and photos into one file and saves it to a location on your device that you choose. You can bring it back with "Restore backup". This file is not sent anywhere unless you move or share it yourself. When you share a diary as a picture, the picture is handed only to the app you pick and is not sent to the developer.

You can optionally lock the app with a 4-digit password. The password is stored on your device only, in a form that cannot be reversed, and is never sent anywhere.

Home screen widgets only display diaries and photos stored on your device on that same device's home screen. They send nothing outside.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads. To show ads it uses the Google AdMob service, and in that process Google may collect and use the following information:

  • Advertising ID (advertising identifier)
  • IP address, device information (model, OS version, language, etc.) and app usage information

This information is used to deliver ads, measure ad performance and prevent invalid clicks, and is handled under the Google Privacy Policy. Your diary content and photos are not used for ads. You can delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Settings > Google > Ads.

The app offers an in-app purchase ("Remove ads") that turns ads off. The payment is handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store card numbers or other payment details, and the app only checks on your device whether the purchase was made.

3. Sharing and retention

The developer does not keep your personal information or provide it to third parties. Information related to ads is handled by Google as described in section 2, under Google's own policy.

4. Children

The app is not directed mainly at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the changes and the new effective date will be posted on this page.
